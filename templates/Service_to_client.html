Key Client Partnerships & Raw Material Supply

GSL / GHCL Supply Operations

We support large-scale chemical manufacturing operations by supplying key process inputs:

  • Industrial Salt: High-purity grade suited for chemical processing, chlor-alkali units, and brine synthesis.
  • Soda Ash (Light & Dense): Superior chemical purity for industrial detergent formulations, glass production, and chemical processing.
  • Industrial Coal & Lignite: High Gross Calorific Value (GCV) thermal coal with controlled moisture and low ash content for industrial boilers and captive power units.

Why Choose Us as Your Supply Partner?

  • Key Industry Support: Proven experience fulfilling raw material demands for sector leaders like GSL and RSPL Group.
  • Strict Quality Standards: Lab-tested shipments ensuring consistent chemical purity and optimal moisture control.
  • Seamless Logistics & Delivery: Streamlined bulk transportation network guaranteeing on-time plant-site supply.
  • Scalable Order Quantities: Flexible bulk contracts ranging from truckloads to regular containerized delivery schedules.
