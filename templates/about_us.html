About Bhagirath Group

Delivering excellence in Transportation, Real Estate Building Materials, River Sand, and Stone Aggregates across Gujarat since 1988.

Company Profile & Origin

Bhagirath Group is built on strong business ethics, integrity, and a commitment to reliable service. It was founded by Karshanbhai (Amubhai) Solanki, who started the business at the age of 32.

Born and raised in a village in Gujarat, Karshanbhai came from a farming family and worked as a farmer in his early years. With access to substantial agricultural land and a natural entrepreneurial mindset, he began undertaking contract work for sugar factories, where he identified a strong opportunity in transport and logistics services.

On April 1, 1988, Karshanbhai established his own transportation business. Through persistence, hard work, disciplined operations, and sharp business insight, he developed Bhagirath into a trusted provider of high-quality transportation services for leading companies and factories in and around Veraval, Gujarat.

Karshanbhai (Amubhai) Solanki — Founder

Growth & Business Expansion

Over the years, Bhagirath Group expanded beyond transportation into a diversified enterprise serving industrial, infrastructure, and construction needs across Gujarat. Today, the group provides excellence across several vertical markets:

  • 🚛 Transportation and Logistics
  • 🏢 Real Estate & Commercial Hubs
  • 🏗️ Premium Building Materials
  • 💎 River Sand & Stone Aggregates

The group's logistics operations include Bhagirath Roadways and associated companies, which have provided transport, loading, and freight services since 1988. Its foundational accomplishments include strategic transportation and loading services for GHCL, a premier soda ash manufacturer operating out of Sutrapada, Veraval.

Our Founder's Legacy

Karshanbhai Solanki’s journey from farming to enterprise reflects the group’s core values: hard work, transparency, customer trust, and long-term relationships. Under his continued leadership vision, Bhagirath Group remains a trusted Gujarat-based enterprise recognized for dependable industrial distribution and logistics solutions.
