Mining Operation

River Sand Mining and Stone Aggregate Quarrying.

Sustainable Mining & Material Extraction

Bhagirath Group operates advanced, environmentally compliant mining and quarrying units across the region. We handle the complete process from professional extraction to precision processing, ensuring high-grade raw supplies for core infrastructure development.

🌾

Division 1: River Sand Mining

Our riverbed dredging and sand mining operations are executed using modern eco-compliant machinery. The raw extracted alluvial sand goes through extensive washing and systematic screening arrays to clear away all organic silts, heavy clays, and chemical impurities. This produces specialized structural fractions:

  • • Fine Plastering Sand: Screened ultra-fine grains sorted precisely to ensure smooth, crack-resistant interior and exterior masonry plaster finishes.
  • • Coarse Concrete Sand: Heavy, washed granules providing the high-shear strength grading required for durable concrete mixes and structural foundation fills.
  • • Bricklaying Masonry Sand: Specially graded sand offering excellent moisture retention and peak workability for mortar compounds.
🏗️

Division 2: Stone Aggregate Quarrying

Our open-cast stone quarries harvest high-density basalt rock reserves. Extracted boulders feed into multi-stage mechanical jaw crushers and vertical shaft impactors (VSI). The crushed stone is systematically divided into precise cubical dimensions to meet heavy structural weight limits:

  • • 10mm Down Aggregates: Sized for thin pre-cast concrete installations, roofing panels, specialized paving blocks, and smooth surface mixes.
  • • 20mm Standard Aggregates: The core product required for ready-mix concrete (RMC) configurations, high-rise pillars, slabs, and beam structures.
  • • 40mm & Sub-Base Ballast: Large angular stone fractions used primarily for structural site grading foundations, drainage layers, and railway bedding.

Our Products & Services

1. Premium River Sand

Our natural river sand is meticulously sourced and processed to ensure the highest purity and optimal grain distribution. It is completely free from organic impurities, clay particles, and harmful chemical contents, ensuring perfect structural cohesion.

  • • Plastering Sand: Ultra-fine grain texture ideal for creating ultra-smooth, crack-free interior and exterior wall plaster finishes.
  • • Concrete Sand: Coarse, washed river sand engineered specifically for heavy-duty concrete mixing and robust foundation work.
  • • Bricklaying Sand: High-grade masonry sand providing exceptional workability and strong bonding properties for brick and block work.

2. Heavy-Duty Stone Aggregates

Our crushed stone aggregates are manufactured from high-quality basalt or granite boulders. They are mechanically crushed and precisely screened into uniform sizes to provide maximum structural load-bearing capacity.

  • • 10mm Aggregates: Perfect for thin concrete slabs, pre-cast concrete structures, roofing elements, and smooth surface finishes.
  • • 20mm Aggregates: The industry standard size for ready-mix concrete (RMC), foundational slabs, beams, columns, and general structural framework.
  • • 40mm & Larger Aggregates: Heavy-duty crushed stone ideal for bulk concrete foundations, railway ballast, road base layers, and drainage systems.
